Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings > Delete Account. If you can't reach Settings (for example, while your organization is awaiting approval or on the subscription screen), use the Delete Account button on that screen. Deleting your account permanently deletes your account and the content you created. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Report content or block a user

Use Report on a post, message, or user to email us a report, or use Block on a user, a conversation, or a Resources post to stop seeing someone and stop their message requests. We review every report within 24 hours. See the Terms of Service.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. To change or cancel one, use your Apple account's subscription settings or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Deleting your HolyGuard account does not cancel a subscription.
