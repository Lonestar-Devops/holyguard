# Privacy Policy for HolyGuard

**Last Updated: November 10, 2026**

## Introduction

HolyGuard ("we," "our," or "us") operates a mobile security application for faith communities and organizations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, assigned role (admin, security, member)
- **Organization Information**: Church/organization name, address, and location coordinates, plus any details an organization provides when it registers (such as EIN, website, phone number, and organization email)
- **Authentication Data**: Password (encrypted), email verification status
- **Subscription Information**: For organizations that subscribe, the plan purchased and the App Store transaction details needed to keep it active (product, transaction identifiers, and renewal or expiration dates). We do not receive your payment card or billing details.
- **Block List**: The users you have chosen to block

### User-Generated Content
- **Security Reports**: Photos, descriptions, timestamps, and location data for incident reports
- **Team Messages**: Text communications with other members of your organization
- **Direct Messages**: Messages and photos you send to other HolyGuard users, and the message requests you send or receive
- **Resources Posts**: Titles, descriptions, categories, and photos you post to Resources
- **Watchlist Entries**: Information your organization records about persons of interest, which may include a name or alias, photos, license plate, physical description, location, and notes (see Watchlist below)

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, and IP address, which our service providers receive when your device connects to them
- **Usage Data**: Records of certain account and administrative actions (for example, organization approvals and changes to an organization's details), kept for security
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates when submitting incident reports. If you tap Find training near me on the Resources tab, your current location is used to search for nearby training locations; it is sent to Google Places for that search and is not stored by HolyGuard.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the HolyGuard service
- Enable real-time security alerts and team communication within your organization
- Provide the network-wide features described below: the network map, the user directory, Resources, and direct messages between users
- Display your organization's location on the network map
- Verify new organizations and authenticate users to prevent unauthorized access
- Verify email addresses for account security
- Activate and maintain your organization's subscription
- Review reports of objectionable content and enforce our Terms of Service
- Improve app functionality and user experience

## Data Storage and Security

- **Cloud Storage**: Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase and Google Cloud Platform
- **Encryption**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Access Control**: Security alerts, team messages, and Watchlist entries can be accessed only by authenticated members of your organization. Information used by the network-wide features is visible to other HolyGuard users as described under Data Sharing and Disclosure.
- **Backups**: Data is backed up regularly to prevent loss

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### Within Your Organization
- Security alerts, team messages, and Watchlist entries are shared with other authenticated members of your organization
- Your name and role are visible to other members of your organization
- Organization admins can remove members and delete team messages in their organization's channels

### Across the HolyGuard Network
Some HolyGuard features connect users in different organizations. Information in these features is visible to signed-in HolyGuard users outside your organization:
- **Network Map**: Your organization's name, address, and location, and its phone number and website if provided, appear on the network map for all HolyGuard users. Other signed-in users can also access the other details stored with your organization's profile, such as its EIN, organization email, and subscription status.
- **User Directory**: The directory lists HolyGuard users in all organizations with their name, role, and organization. Other signed-in users can also access your email address through the directory.
- **Resources**: Resources posts, including their photos and the name and organization of the person who posted them, are visible to all HolyGuard users
- **Direct Messages**: Any HolyGuard user, including users in other organizations, can send you a message request. If you accept, the messages and photos in that conversation are visible to the two participants.

### Watchlist
Organizations can use the Watchlist to record persons of interest involved in security incidents at their site. Watchlist entries may include information about people who are not HolyGuard users, such as a name or alias, photos, license plate, physical description, location, and notes. Watchlist entries are visible only to members of the organization that created them and can be deleted by the member who created them or by an admin of that organization. Organizations are responsible for recording only accurate information about people involved in an actual incident. If you believe a Watchlist entry is about you, contact us at support@cowboystateops.com.

### We Do NOT Share
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties
- We do not share your data with advertisers
- Other than the network-wide features described above, we do not share your organization's data with other organizations

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required to:
- Comply with legal obligations or court orders
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of HolyGuard, our users, or the public
- Investigate and prevent fraudulent or illegal activities

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- **View Data**: Access your profile information within the app
- **Update Data**: Change your password in Settings. To correct your name or email address, contact us.
- **Delete Account**: Delete your account at any time from inside the app, as described below

### Deleting Your Account
You can delete your account in the app at **Settings > Delete Account**. If you are signed in but cannot reach Settings (for example, while your organization is awaiting approval, on the subscription screen, or before you accept the Terms), use the **Delete Account** button on that screen. For your security, you will be asked to enter your password again.

Deleting your account permanently deletes your sign-in account, your profile, and the content you created, including your incident reports, team messages, direct messages and message requests, Resources posts, Watchlist entries, and the photos you uploaded.

If you are the only admin of an organization that still has other members, the app asks you to choose one of those members as the new admin before your account is deleted.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. To cancel, use your Apple account's subscription settings on your device or visit https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

Your organization's profile (its name, address, and the other details shown on the network map) is not deleted when one member deletes their account. If you are the last member of your organization and want it removed, email support@cowboystateops.com.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@cowboystateops.com.

### Reporting Content and Blocking Users
- **Report**: Use Report on a Resources post, a direct message or conversation, a user in the directory, or a team message. This opens a pre-filled email to support@cowboystateops.com that identifies the content and the user involved. We use reports only to review and act on them, and we review every report within 24 hours.
- **Block**: Use Block on a user in the directory, a conversation, or a Resources post to block that user. You will no longer see that user in the user directory, direct messages, or Resources, and neither of you can send the other message requests or messages. The blocked user is not notified. You can review and unblock users in Settings > Blocked Users.

### Location Data
- You can disable location services in your device settings, but this will limit incident reporting and the nearby training search on the Resources tab

### Communications
- You control which team messages you send
- You can decline direct message requests and block users
- You can log out at any time

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: We retain your data while your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: When you delete your account in the app, your account and the content described under Deleting Your Account are deleted right away. If you ask us by email, we delete your account and data within 30 days.
- **Backups**: Deleted data may persist in backups for up to 90 days

## Children's Privacy

HolyGuard is intended only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to use HolyGuard. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately at support@cowboystateops.com.

## Third-Party Services

HolyGuard uses the following third-party services:
- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud Platform**: Authentication, database, file storage, and server functions
- **Google Maps Platform (Places and Geocoding)**: Address lookup when an organization registers or updates its address, organization verification, and the nearby training search on the Resources tab
- **Mapbox**: Displays the network map. To load the map, your device connects to Mapbox, which receives your IP address and device information and may collect usage data as described in its own privacy policy.
- **Apple App Store (StoreKit)**: Subscriptions are purchased, renewed, and cancelled through Apple. Apple processes your payment; we receive only the transaction details described above, including Apple's notices about renewals, cancellations, and refunds.
- **ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer**: If an organization provides an EIN when it registers, we look the EIN up in this public nonprofit database to help verify the organization

These services have their own privacy policies governing their use of your information.
